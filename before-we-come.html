Before we come

A short checklist for a booked pickup, in the order you will need it. A little time the day before means we arrive, look, load, sweep and go, with no waiting while a closet gets sorted.

Set out what goes

  • Mark what stays. Walk the space with a roll of tape and mark what you want to keep, or move it to another room. If what goes is the shorter list, mark that instead.
  • Empty the drawers. Take everything out of dressers, desks and filing cabinets. A full dresser is far heavier to carry, and your papers stay with you.
  • Bag the loose things. Small items go faster in bags or boxes than one at a time.
  • Defrost a fridge or freezer at least a day ahead, with towels underneath.
  • Look at the pile next to what you booked. If there is more than the size you picked or the photos you sent, call or text us before the day.

Clear the path

From the pile to the door to the truck, move anything we could trip on or scratch.

  • Doors and gates. Unlock the gate, and put away anything in the yard that should not be moved.
  • Stairs and halls. Clear the stairs and the hall. If there is a tight turn or a narrow hall, tell us when you book so we come ready for it.
  • The elevator. In an apartment or condo, reserve the freight elevator if your building has one, and let the front desk know we are coming. In larger buildings, such as downtown Portland or near Orenco in Hillsboro, that one call can save a long wait on the day.

Parking

  • Leave room close to the door. A spot in the driveway or at the curb in front saves carrying time, so move a car out of the way the night before if you can.
  • A busy street. If the only space is on a busy street, tell us when you book.
  • Apartments. Check where trucks are allowed to stop, such as a loading zone, and tell us when you book.
  • Permits. Most streets need no permit for a short stop. If yours does, tell us when you book and we work it out with you before the day.

Pets and children

Keep children and pets away from the path while we carry heavy things. A door held open for carrying is an easy way out for a pet, so a closed room away from the path is the safest place. If a pet will be in the yard, tell us when you book.

If you will not be home

You do not have to be.

  • Set it outside. Put everything in the driveway or under a carport, and tell us where it is in the notes for the crew when you book.
  • In the garage? Leave a door code, or a key in a lockbox, in the same notes.
  • Inside the house? Then someone needs to let us in.
  • More than you booked? We text you the new price before we load it, and we load the bigger size only if you agree.
  • Photos when we finish. We send photos when the job is done, so you can see the space is clear.

What we cannot take

These need special handling, so they cannot ride with the load. Set them aside before we arrive:

  • Paint, stains and solvents. Empty paint cans that are dried out are fine.
  • Chemicals, such as pool chemicals, pesticides and drain cleaner.
  • Fuel, propane tanks and lighter fluid.
  • Asbestos and medical waste.

If we find any of these in the pile, we set them aside and tell you. What junk haulers can't take, and where it goes has the full list and where each one can go instead.

On the day

  1. The day before, we send a reminder with your arrival window.
  2. On our way. We text you when the crew leaves for your place.
  3. Inside your window. A crew of two arrives within the arrival window you picked.
  4. The walk through. Before we lift anything, we walk the pile with you and check the size. If it is bigger than you booked, we show you the new price first and charge the difference only if you agree. If it is smaller, we refund the difference.
  5. Load, sweep and go. We carry everything out, load it and sweep the spot where it stood. Most pickups take under an hour. Anything still usable goes to donation, metal and electronics go to recycling, and the rest goes to the transfer station.
  6. Your receipt. We email it after the job.

Need another day? Call or text us. Until 24 hours before your arrival window, you can move your day for free, or cancel and we refund you in full. After that, a cancel keeps the late fee of $50.00 and we refund the rest.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Brand Name ("we", "us") is a junk removal business based in Gresham, Oregon. This page says what we collect when you get a price or book with us, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email and the address of the job.
  • The job: the items or load size you picked, your ZIP code and travel zone, the day you chose, and any notes you give us.
  • Photos: the photos you send for a quote, and the before and after photos we take on the job.
  • Payment: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • How the site is used: standard server logs and analytics events, such as pages viewed and buttons tapped. No advertising trackers.

Photos you send us

When you send photos for a quote, we use an AI model made by Anthropic to count the items in them, so the truck on the page fills for you. The photos are sent to Anthropic only for that count. We keep the photos in private storage with your quote, and if you book, with your job, so we can see what we are hauling before we arrive. Photos from a quote that never became a booking are deleted after 90 days.

How we use it

  • To price and schedule the job you asked for, and to do it.
  • To send your confirmation, reminder, arrival window and photos when we finish.
  • To take payment and send receipts.
  • To follow up once on a quote you did not book, by email or text, unless you ask us not to.
  • To answer your questions and do a better job.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the business, and only what each one needs: Stripe (payments), Supabase (our database and photo storage), Resend (email), Anthropic (counting items in quote photos), and Google Analytics (how the site is used). None of them may use your information for their own marketing. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we have about you, correct it, or delete it, at any time. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Ask us to delete your quote photos and we will. If you live in Washington, you have the same rights; we do not collect health data of any kind.

How long we keep it

We keep job records, receipts and job photos for three years for our taxes and in case a question comes up about a job. Then we delete them.

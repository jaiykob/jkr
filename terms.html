Terms of service

Last updated: October 11, 2026

These terms are the agreement between you and Haulmighty when you book a haul or a monthly pickup. They are written in plain English on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you book.

Who we are

Haulmighty is a junk removal business based in Gresham, Oregon, serving the Portland metro and Clark County, Washington. It is a sole proprietorship run by its owner, who also does the work. We work out of a truck and have no storefront you can visit. Call or text (925) 408-6053 or email hello@example.com.

Agreeing to these terms

You agree to these terms when you book a haul, start a monthly pickup, or let us take items from a property. You must be at least 18, and you must own the items and the property or have the owner's permission to have them hauled away.

Where we work

We work in every ZIP code inside our travel zones: the Portland metro in Oregon and the Clark County side of the river in Washington, out to 40 road miles from our base in Gresham. A ZIP past the last zone cannot be booked online; call or text and we will quote it by hand. If a booking turns out to be outside our area, we tell you and refund you in full rather than send a truck.

What a haul includes

  • Carrying the items out from where they are on the property.
  • Loading them, hauling them away, and sweeping the spot they came from.
  • Donating what is still usable and recycling what we can.
  • Photos when the job is done, if you were not there to see it.

What we can't take

We do not take paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos or medical waste. If we find any of these in a load, we set them aside, leave them with you, and tell you where they can go. We may also refuse anything we cannot lift or carry safely.

Prices, travel and changes on the day

Your price is the price for the load size you booked plus the travel fee for your ZIP code's zone. The load size comes from the size card you picked or the photos you sent. The price on your quote and your confirmation is the price you pay, as long as the job matches the size you booked.

If the load is a different size when we arrive than the one you booked, we show you the new load size and price before we load anything. If it is bigger, you can accept the new price or take items off so it fits the size you booked. We charge the difference only after you agree. If you decide not to go ahead when we arrive, we cancel the visit and refund your payment less a refusal fee of $0.00. We never load first and charge more later.

If the load is smaller than the size you booked, we price the smaller load size and refund the difference.

Paying

We take cards through Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number. You get an emailed receipt for every charge and refund.

  • When you book, you pay the price you were quoted: your load size plus travel for your ZIP code. Your card is saved so we can settle a different size.
  • After the job, if the load was a different size than the one you booked, we charge or refund the difference on that card.
  • By booking you agree to these terms, and to your card being charged or refunded the difference if the crew finds a different size.

If a card is declined for the difference after a job, we email you a secure link to pay it. Regular pickups for property managers and businesses are arranged with us directly through the property manager form.

Your day and getting to the items

You choose the day and an arrival window when you book, and we text you when the crew is on the way. You do not need to be home if the items are outside or we have a way in. If we arrive and cannot reach the items because of a locked gate, a blocked driveway or no one to let us in, we call you; if we cannot reach you within 30 minutes, the visit counts as a cancellation on the day.

We may move a job for ice, snow or a storm, and we tell you as early as we can.

Cancelling and changing

Change or cancel by call, text or email. Until 24 hours before your arrival window starts, you can move your day for free, or cancel and we refund you in full. After that, a cancel keeps the late fee of $50.00 and we refund the rest.

Refunds

  • A haul you cancel at least 24 hours before your arrival window: refunded in full.
  • A haul you cancel inside that time: refunded less the late fee of $50.00.
  • A load smaller than the size you booked: the difference is refunded after the job.
  • A job you decide not to go ahead with when we arrive: refunded less the refusal fee of $0.00.

If you think a charge is wrong, contact us before disputing it with your bank. We would rather fix it directly, and we usually can the same day.

What we are responsible for

We carry general liability insurance. If we damage something and it is our fault, tell us within seven days and send photos. We will repair it, replace it, or pay the reasonable cost, up to the price of the job for anything that is not covered by our insurance.

We are not responsible for:

  • Damage that was there before we arrived.
  • Wear on a floor, lawn or driveway from carrying heavy items the normal way.
  • Items you asked us to take that you later want back. Once a load leaves, we cannot get things back from a donation center or a transfer station.
  • Anything inside furniture or boxes that you did not take out first.

Messages we send you

When you give us your email and phone number, you agree to receive messages about your job: the booking confirmation, a reminder, the arrival window, photos when we finish, and billing notices. Reply STOP to stop texts; you will still get emails about your booking and billing, because those are part of the service. We do not send marketing texts.

Photos

The photos you send for a quote, and the before and after photos we take, are kept with your job as a record of what was there and what we took. We will not publish a photo that shows your home or your family without asking you first.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The version on this page when you book is the one that applies to that booking.

The law that applies

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Oregon. A job in Washington is also subject to Washington law where it applies.

Price questions

Pricing questions and answers

Clear prices for junk removal across Portland and Vancouver.

001How much does junk removal cost in Portland?

One flat price per load, by how much of the truck your stuff fills:

  • Single item: $99.00
  • 1/4 truck: $199.00
  • 1/2 truck: $349.00
  • 3/4 truck: $479.00
  • Full truck: $599.00

Travel for your ZIP is added on top. More than a full truck? Call or text and we price it for you.

002Are there any hidden fees?

No. Your total is the load price plus travel for your ZIP, and you see both before you book. The load price covers the labor, the loading and the haul.

The one other charge is for a late cancel: until 24 hours before your arrival window you can move your day for free, or cancel and get a full refund. After that, a cancel keeps a fee of $50.00.

003How does the travel fee work?

Travel is set by your ZIP, in rings of road miles from our base in Gresham:

  • Home zone, up to 10 road miles: no charge
  • Zone 2, 10 to 20 road miles: +$25.00
  • Zone 3, 20 to 30 road miles: +$50.00
  • Zone 4, 30 to 40 road miles: +$75.00
  • Past 40 road miles: call or text and we price it for you

Type your ZIP in the calculator above to see your zone.

004What if my pile is bigger or smaller than I booked?

The crew checks the size with you before loading. If it is bigger, we show you the new price first and charge the difference only if you agree. If it is smaller, we refund the difference.

005When do I pay?

Online when you book, so your day is held for you. After the job, any change in size is settled as above, and we email your receipt.

006Can I get a price from photos?

Yes. Send up to four photos of what is going. We count the items, pick the size and show your price with travel. No estimate visit.

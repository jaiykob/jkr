Getting a price

How do I get a price?

Pick the size that looks like your pile, or send a few photos and we pick it for you. Add your ZIP and the price includes travel. No call, no visit to look at it first.

Can I just send a photo?

Yes. Send up to four photos of the things you want gone. We count the items in them and pick the load size for you, and you can change it if it looks wrong. The count only picks the load size; the price comes from our price list, the same for everyone.

What if I have more than I booked?

When we arrive we look at the job before we load anything. If it is a different size than you booked, we show you the new load size and price first. If it is bigger, we charge the difference to the card you booked with, only after you agree. If it is smaller, we refund the difference. If you decide not to go ahead when we arrive, we refund your payment less a refusal fee of $0.00. We never load first and tell you later.

How much does junk removal cost?

You pay for the space your things take in the truck, from a single item up to a full truck. Labor, loading and the haul are all in that price. The price list shows every load size, and travel is added by zone.

Is there a minimum charge?

The smallest job is a single item, like one couch or one mattress, and its price is on the price list. Travel for your zone is added to it.

Where we go

Why is there a travel charge?

It covers the drive. We are based in Gresham, and travel is set by your ZIP code in rings of road miles. Close to Gresham there is no travel charge; each ring farther out adds a flat amount to the visit. Type your ZIP on the homepage or in the quote to see your zone and what it adds.

Do you come to my area?

We cover the whole Portland metro, from Gresham and Sandy to Hillsboro and Wilsonville, and across the river to Vancouver, Camas and Battle Ground. See every town we serve. If your ZIP is past our last travel zone, call or text and we will quote it by hand.

Do you work in Washington?

Yes. We cross the I-205 and I-5 bridges to Vancouver, Camas, Washougal, Battle Ground and the rest of Clark County, at the same load prices as Oregon plus the travel for your ZIP.

On the day

Do I have to be home?

No. Leave the items outside, in the driveway or the garage, and tell us where to find them when you book. Your card is on file, and we send photos when the job is done. If the items are inside, someone needs to let us in, or leave a key in a lockbox and tell us where in the notes. See if you will not be home.

Do you do all the lifting?

Yes. We carry everything out from wherever it sits, load it, and sweep up the spot it came from. Stairs and long carries are included in the price.

How soon can you come?

You pick the day and a two hour arrival window when you book. Most weeks we have a day open within two or three days. On the day we text you when the crew is on the way.

Do you still come if it rains?

Yes, we haul in the rain. Ice, snow or a storm can push a job to the next open day, and we tell you as early as we can.

What we take

What do you take?

Furniture, mattresses and box springs, fridges, freezers, washers and dryers, TVs and electronics, carpet, boxes and bags, yard debris, hot tubs, and garage, basement and estate cleanouts. If you can point to it and we can lift it, we can usually take it.

What can't you take?

We can't take paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos or medical waste. If we find any of these in a load we set them aside and tell you, and we can point you to the right drop off.

Where does my junk go?

Anything still usable goes to donation. Metal, electronics and cardboard go to recycling. The rest goes to a licensed transfer station, such as Metro Central in Portland or Metro South in Oregon City.

Can you take my old fridge?

Yes. Fridges, freezers and air conditioners go to a recycler licensed to remove the refrigerant. Empty them before we come.

Paying and changes

How do I pay?

By card when you book, through Stripe, for the price you were quoted. We save the card so that, if the job turns out a different size, we can charge or refund the difference after the job. We never see or store your full card number. You get an email receipt for every charge.

Can I cancel or change my day?

Yes. Call, text or email us. Until 24 hours before your arrival window, you can move your day for free, or cancel and we refund you in full. After that, a cancel keeps the late fee of $50.00 and we refund the rest.

Can you come back every month?

Yes, for landlords, property managers and businesses. Set up regular pickups through our property manager form. Travel for your zone is part of each visit.

A good pickup is quick: we arrive, look, load, sweep and go. A little preparation the day before makes the difference between a twenty minute visit and an hour of waiting while a closet gets sorted. Here is the checklist we would use for our own house.

## The day before

- **Pull out anything that stays.** Walk the space with a roll of tape and mark anything you want to keep, or move it to another room.
- **Take things out of drawers.** Empty dressers, desks and filing cabinets. A dresser full of clothes is three times as heavy, and nobody wants a stranger handling their papers.
- **Bag the loose stuff.** Small items go faster in bags or boxes than one at a time.
- **Set aside the items we cannot take**: paint, chemicals, fuel and propane. The full list is in our [FAQ](/faq.html).
- **Defrost a fridge or freezer** at least a day ahead, with towels underneath.

## The morning of

- **Clear a path.** From the pile to the door to the driveway, move anything we could trip on or scratch.
- **Make room to park.** A spot close to the door saves carrying time. If the only space is on a busy street, tell us when you book.
- **Unlock the gate** and put away anything in the yard that should not be disturbed.
- **Keep kids and pets away from the path** while we carry heavy things.

## If you will not be home

You do not have to be. Set everything outside, in the driveway or under a carport, and tell us where it is when you book. If it is in the garage, leave a code or a key in a lockbox. We send photos when the job is done so you can see the space is clear.

## Compare the pile to your quote

Before we arrive, glance at the pile against the photos or items in your booking. If there is more, add it to your quote or tell us. On the day, if the load is bigger than you showed us, we show you the new price before we load anything, and you choose what goes.

## Apartments and condos

Reserve the freight elevator if your building has one, check where trucks are allowed to stop, and let the front desk know we are coming. In larger buildings, such as downtown [Portland](/portland.html) or near Orenco in [Hillsboro](/hillsboro.html), that one phone call can save a long wait on the day.

Most people ask the same first question: what is this going to cost me? The honest answer is that it depends on two things, and only two: how much of the truck your things fill, and how far the truck has to drive to reach you. Everything else is built into the price.

## You pay for space, not for weight or time

Junk removal is priced by volume. Picture the back of our truck as a box, and picture your couch, mattress and boxes stacked inside it. One couch takes a slice of that box. A full garage might take all of it. The price list on our homepage shows each step, from a single item up to a full truck, and you can see it any time on the [price list](/index.html#pricing).

That flat price covers the labor, the loading and the haul. We do the carrying, whether the item is in the driveway, the basement or a second floor bedroom. There is no hourly meter running while we work, so a tight staircase does not quietly add to your bill.

## Travel is set by your ZIP code

We are based in Gresham, and travel is added by zone: rings of road miles out from our base. Close in, around [Gresham](/gresham.html), [Troutdale](/troutdale.html) and parts of east Portland, there is no travel charge at all. Farther out, each ring adds a flat amount to the visit. When you type your ZIP code into the quote, the price you see already includes it.

We put travel on its own line on purpose. Two neighbors with the same pile in different towns should understand why their prices differ, and a flat zone fee is easier to trust than a mystery surcharge on the day.

## What changes the price

- **More than you showed us.** If there is more on the day than your photos or taps showed, we show you the new load size and price before we load anything. You decide.
- **Things we cannot take.** Paint, chemicals and fuel stay with you, so they do not count toward the load.
- **Very heavy material.** [[Loose soil, concrete and roofing shingles are priced by hand]], because a truck reaches its weight limit long before it runs out of space.

Nothing else changes it. Not the stairs, not the rain, not the time of year.

## How to know your price before anyone comes out

The fastest way is to snap a few photos on our [quote page](/book/quote.html). We count the items in them and pick the load size for you, and you can change it if it looks wrong. Prefer to choose yourself? Pick the size card that looks like your pile. Either way, the price shows up with the size, and travel joins it as soon as you add your ZIP.

## Why online pricing instead of an estimate visit

Many haulers send someone out to look first, then quote. That costs you an appointment and costs them a trip, and someone pays for that trip eventually. Showing the price up front lets you decide on your own time, compare fairly, and book a day without a phone call. If you would still rather talk it through, call or text us and we will walk through it with you.

A mattress is one of the hardest things in a house to get rid of on your own. It is big, floppy, heavy for its size, and most cars cannot carry one. Here are the real options, from cheapest to easiest.

## Can it be donated?

Only if it is in very good shape. Most donation centers in the area [[no longer accept used mattresses]] because of bedbug and hygiene rules, and the ones that do want no stains, no tears and no sagging. If yours qualifies, call ahead before you load it.

## Mattress recycling

A mattress is mostly steel springs, foam and fabric, and nearly all of it can be recycled. [[Oregon now has a statewide mattress recycling program with drop off sites]], usually at transfer stations and some retailers. If you have a truck or a large SUV and an afternoon, dropping it off yourself is the lowest cost route.

## Curbside pickup

Garbage haulers in the metro [[will take a mattress as a bulky item for an extra fee if you schedule it]]. The catch is the scheduling, the wait, and the rule that it has to be at the curb by a certain time. A mattress left out in the rain overnight gets heavy and is harder for anyone to move.

## Getting it ready

- Strip the bedding and take anything out from under the bed.
- If it is upstairs, clear a path down the stairs.
- Tell us if there is a tight turn or a narrow hall; we bring straps and a dolly.
- If you suspect bedbugs, tell us when you book so we can bag it before carrying it through the house.

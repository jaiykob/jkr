Both get rid of a pile. The difference is who does the work, how long it takes, and what the real total looks like when everything is counted. Here is a fair comparison.

## How a dumpster works

A company drops a container in your driveway, you fill it over several days, and they haul it away. You pay for the size of the container and the rental period, and [[often an overweight fee if the load is heavier than the container's limit]]. Some items, like mattresses and appliances, [[may cost extra or not be allowed at all]].

## How junk removal works

We come at a set time, carry everything out from wherever it sits, load it, sweep up and leave. You pay a flat price for the space your things take in the truck, plus travel for your zone, and you see that price before you book on our [quote page](/book/quote.html).

## When a dumpster makes sense

- **A long remodel** that produces debris every day for weeks.
- **Heavy material** like roofing, concrete or demolition debris, which is priced by weight.
- **You want to load it yourself**, at your own pace, and have the help to do it.

## When junk removal makes sense

- **The things are inside.** Carrying a couch down two flights is our job, not yours.
- **You want it done today.** One visit instead of a container sitting out for a week.
- **There is no good place for a container.** Many driveways are too steep or too short, and a dumpster on the street [[can need a city permit]].
- **You want your driveway back.** A heavy container can leave marks on asphalt.
- **The load is mixed.** Furniture, appliances, electronics and bags get sorted for donation and recycling instead of all going to the landfill.

## The real total

For a dumpster, add the rental, any overweight fee, any permit, and your own weekend of loading. For junk removal, the price you see is the price you pay, as long as the load matches what you showed us. If it turns out bigger, we show you the new price before we load anything.

## A common middle path

Contractors in places like [Wilsonville](/wilsonville.html) and [West Linn](/west-linn.html) often use a dumpster for the demolition and call us for the furniture, the old appliances and the cleanup at the end. Use each one for what it does best.

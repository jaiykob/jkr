A full garage builds up slowly, one box at a time, until the car lives in the driveway. The good news is that clearing it is a weekend project if you plan the two days right. This is the plan we suggest to customers.

## Friday night: book the truck

Book the haul for Sunday afternoon before you start. It sounds backward, but a fixed pickup gives the weekend a deadline, and the pile cannot creep back into the garage on Monday. Take a quick photo of the garage as it is and send it with your quote; you can change the load size later if the pile grows or shrinks.

## Saturday morning: four piles

Pull everything out onto the driveway and sort into four piles:

- **Keep**: things you used in the last year, or truly need.
- **Sell**: anything worth the trouble of a listing.
- **Donate**: usable things you are done with.
- **Go**: broken, worn out or not worth anyone's time.

Be strict with the keep pile. If a box has been taped shut since the last move, open it once, and then decide.

## Saturday afternoon: the hard stuff

Pull out the paint, chemicals, fuel and propane; those need their own trip (see [what haulers can't take](/blog/what-junk-haulers-cant-take.html)). Drain the gas from the old mower. Flatten the boxes. Bag loose screws and parts.

## Sunday: shelves, then the haul

Put up shelving or hooks for the keep pile before it goes back in: vertical storage is what keeps a garage clear. When we arrive, the donate and go piles both come with us; we take the donations to a thrift store and the rest to recycling or the transfer station.

## What usually fills a load

In our area the same things turn up again and again: old bikes and toys, exercise machines, broken patio sets, scrap wood, a second fridge, carpet rolls, and boxes from three moves ago. Big lots in places like [Damascus](/damascus.html) and [Boring](/boring.html) add shop tools and fencing.

## How big a load?

Most single car garages fit in half a truck once they are sorted; a packed two car garage is often a full truck.

There are many ways to get a pile hauled in the Portland metro, from national franchises to a neighbor with a pickup. Prices and service vary widely. These six questions sort them out quickly.

## 1. How is the price set, and when do I see it?

The best answer is a clear price before anyone shows up. Be careful with "starting at" prices and estimates that only become real once the truck is in your driveway. Ask what the price includes: labor, loading, the haul and disposal should all be in it.

## 2. What happens if there is more than I showed you?

Things get missed; a basement corner or a second shed turns up on the day. A good company shows you the new price before loading anything and lets you choose. Ours does exactly that, and you can say no.

## 3. Is travel extra?

If you live outside the company's home area, ask how travel is charged. A flat fee by zone is fair and easy to check. We list ours by ZIP code on the [service areas page](/service-areas.html), and it is already in the price on our [quote page](/book/quote.html).

## 4. Where do my things go?

Ask whether usable items are donated and whether metal, electronics and appliances are recycled. A hauler that sends everything straight to the landfill is cheaper to run, not cheaper to the planet.

## 5. Are you insured?

Accidents happen when heavy things move through tight doorways. Ask whether the company carries liability insurance and how a damage claim works. [[We carry general liability insurance]], and our terms say plainly what we cover.

## 6. How do I pay, and when?

Card payment with an emailed receipt is the safest for you. Be wary of cash only and of large deposits for a simple job.

## Red flags

- No written price, only a range on the phone.
- Pressure to decide on the spot once the truck arrives.
- No address, no reviews, no way to reach anyone after the job.

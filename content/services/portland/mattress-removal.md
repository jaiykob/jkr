## What we take
Every kind of bed, in every size from a crib to a California king.

- Innerspring, memory foam and hybrid mattresses
- Box springs and foundations
- Futon mattresses and their frames
- Adjustable bases, [[unplugged and folded flat]]
- Mattress toppers and pillow tops
- Bed frames, headboards, bunk beds and daybeds
- [[Waterbed mattresses, once they are drained]]

A mattress and its box spring count as one item, so a whole bed set is a Single item on the order page. A mattress that is torn or stained can still come. If you think there are bedbugs, tell us when you book, and we wrap it in plastic in the bedroom before carrying it through the house.

## Where it goes
Most of a mattress can be used again once it is taken apart.

- [[Oregon mattresses go through the state's mattress recycling program, where the steel springs go to scrap metal and the foam, cotton and fabric become carpet padding and other products.]]
- [[Washington mattresses go to a Clark County transfer station.]]
- Wooden and metal bed frames go to donation when they are sound, and to scrap or the transfer station when they are not.

Used mattresses almost never go to donation. [[Most charities in the metro stopped taking them because of bedbug and hygiene rules.]]

## Free options
- [[Oregon's mattress recycling program, paid for by a small fee on new mattresses, takes old ones at its drop off sites at no charge.]] It works if you have a truck and an afternoon.
- [[Many stores take the old mattress away when they deliver a new one]], sometimes for a fee, so ask before you buy.
- A clean, nearly new mattress can find a taker on a Buy Nothing group, as long as you are upfront about its age.

If none of those fit, or the mattress is upstairs and you would rather not wrestle it down, a pickup is the easy way.

## How the job goes
Pick Single item for one bed, mattress and box spring together. [[Two beds, or a bed with its frame and a dresser, usually fit a 1/4 truck.]] Send a photo and we choose the size for you.

Strip the sheets and clear out whatever is under the bed. There is no need to bring anything downstairs: we carry it from the bedroom, with straps for the stairs and care for the walls at every turn. Pick a day and a two hour window, and watch for our text when we leave for your place.

When we arrive, we look at the bed or beds with you and confirm the size. If there is more to haul than you booked, you see the new price first and we charge it only if you agree; if there is less, we refund the difference. [[A single mattress pickup usually takes us about fifteen minutes.]]

## FAQ
Q: Does the box spring count as a second item?
A: No. A mattress and its box spring count as one item, so the set is a Single item. Add a frame or a second bed and send a photo to see if it moves up a size.

Q: Can you take a mattress with bedbugs?
A: Yes. Tell us when you book, and we wrap it in plastic in the bedroom before it goes through the house. Leave it on the bed so it is not dragged through other rooms first.

Q: Do I have to carry the mattress outside?
A: No. We take it from wherever it is, up or down the stairs. If you will not be home, leave it inside the garage or by the door and add a note when you book; if we find more than you booked, we text you the price before loading it.

Q: Can you take a mattress out of a storage unit?
A: Yes. Give us the facility, the unit number and the gate code when you book, [[and check that the facility lets outside movers in during your window]].

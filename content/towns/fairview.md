## Local
Fairview is barely four road miles from where we start the day, so it is firmly in our home zone. We haul from the houses around Fairview Lake, the newer blocks in Fairview Village, and the apartments along Halsey Street.

The lake neighborhoods tend to collect outdoor gear: old docks and decking boards, worn out patio furniture, grills and paddle boards. Inside, the usual suspects are mattresses, dressers and the treadmill nobody uses. Point the camera at all of it and the truck fills for you.

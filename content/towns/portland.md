## Local
Portland is where most of our hauls happen, and the city is big enough that travel depends on your side of the river. East Portland neighborhoods like Hazelwood, Lents, Powellhurst and Russell are in our home zone with no travel charge. The inner east side, North Portland, downtown and Hillsdale are in Zone 2, and the far west side, from Multnomah Village up to Cedar Mill, is in Zone 3.

Older Portland houses come with basements, detached garages and narrow driveways, so we bring a dolly and plan the carry before we load anything. Plenty of inner east side streets have no driveway at all; we park at the curb and carry from the porch. [[A short loading stop does not need a parking permit on most residential streets.]]

## FAQ
Q: Can you haul from a Portland house with no driveway?
A: Yes. We park as close as the street allows and carry from the porch, the basement or the alley. If the only space out front is a busy street, tell us when you book so we can plan a quick stop.

Q: Do you haul from condos and apartments downtown?
A: Yes, from the Pearl District to the South Waterfront. [[Most downtown buildings ask you to reserve the freight elevator]], so book it for your day and tell us the time window.

## Local
King City is a small city tucked off Highway 99W near the Tualatin River, about twenty seven road miles from our base, in Zone 3. It began as a retirement community, and many homes are single level with carports, alongside newer neighborhoods on its west side.

Downsizing and moves to care homes make up much of our King City work: a bedroom set, a recliner, a dining table and boxes from the hall closet. Single level homes make these jobs quick, and we take care not to scuff walls or floors on the way out.

## Local
Milwaukie and the Oak Grove area just south of it sit fourteen to sixteen road miles from our base, which puts them in Zone 2. The neighborhoods are a mix of postwar houses with full basements, newer infill near downtown, and bigger lots around Oak Grove.

Basements are the big job here. Decades of storage end up down there: old furniture, boxes of papers, exercise equipment and the water heater that was replaced years ago. We carry it all up the stairs and out, and we can usually finish a full basement in one trip.

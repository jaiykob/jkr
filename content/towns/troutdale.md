## Local
Troutdale sits right at the mouth of the Gorge, a short run up I-84 from our base, which keeps it in the home zone. We pick up from the older homes near the historic downtown, the newer streets climbing toward Strebin Farms, and the townhomes close to the outlet center.

Plenty of Troutdale jobs follow a move: furniture that did not fit the new place, a garage full of boxes, or a basement cleared before a sale. River weekends leave their own leftovers too, like old kayaks, coolers past saving and broken patio sets.

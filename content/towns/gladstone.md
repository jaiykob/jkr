## Local
Gladstone is a small town wedged between the Clackamas and Willamette rivers, about sixteen road miles from our base, in Zone 2. Many of its homes are older single story houses on quiet streets, with detached garages and long back yards.

Riverside living brings yard work, and Gladstone jobs often include piles of branches and brush after a storm along with the usual household items. We also clear out homes for families handling an estate, where everything has to go and the timing matters.

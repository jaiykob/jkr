## Local
Estacada sits on the Clackamas River at the edge of the national forest, nineteen road miles out along Highway 224, which keeps it in Zone 2. Properties here are often larger, with shops, barns and river cabins alongside regular houses in town.

River and forest living fills sheds fast. We haul old camping gear, broken outdoor furniture, scrap wood, and household items from cabins being cleared out or sold. If your property is up a forest road, tell us and send a photo so we can plan the drive.

## Local
Boring is about six road miles from where our truck starts, so it lands in the home zone even though it feels like the country. Properties here run from small houses along Highway 26 to tree farms and acreage out toward Cottrell and Kelso.

We see many shop and outbuilding cleanouts in Boring: workbenches, old engines with the fluids drained, scrap lumber and the odd wood stove. Inside the house it is the same as anywhere, with mattresses, dressers and electronics. We load from the driveway, the barn or the back of the property.

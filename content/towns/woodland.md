## Local
Woodland sits on the Lewis River at the north end of our map, thirty nine road miles from our base, near the outer line of Zone 4. Homes range from houses around Horseshoe Lake to farms and rural lots along the river valley.

Valley farms and older homes bring barn cleanouts, old furniture and outdoor equipment, while lake homes bring docks, boat covers and patio sets. With the drive, we keep Woodland jobs to set days and ask you to send good photos so one trip covers it all.

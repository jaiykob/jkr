## Local
Wood Village is small enough to cross in a couple of minutes and close enough to our base to stay in the home zone. Most of what we haul here comes out of the apartment complexes off Arata Road and the single story houses tucked between Glisan and Halsey.

Because so many Wood Village homes are rentals, move outs are our most common call: a couch left behind, a bed frame, kitchen chairs and a few bags of whatever did not make the cut. Property managers can book the unit and send us the gate or lockbox details.

## FAQ
Q: Can a landlord book a Wood Village move out without being there?
A: Yes. Book online, add the unit number and how we get in, and we will send photos of the empty unit when we leave.

Q: Do you take items left by a tenant who moved out?
A: Yes, as long as you own the property or manage it for the owner. [[If the tenant left personal papers or photos, set them aside first]], since we cannot get anything back once it is hauled.

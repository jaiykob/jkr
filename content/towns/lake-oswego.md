## Local
Lake Oswego sits twenty three to twenty six road miles from our base, in Zone 3. Homes here range from cottages in First Addition to large houses on the hills of Mountain Park and the lakefront lots around Oswego Lake, many with steep driveways and stairs down to the water.

Estate cleanouts and downsizing are a big part of our Lake Oswego work, where a family home of forty years has to be cleared in a weekend. We take furniture, rugs, books and the contents of closets, and we set aside anything you mark to keep. Lakefront homes also bring old dock floats, patio sets and boat covers.

## FAQ
Q: Can you help clear a Lake Oswego home after a family member moves to assisted living?
A: Yes. Walk through with us on the phone or send photos room by room, mark what stays, and we will take the rest. We send photos of each room when we finish.

Q: Can you carry items up from a lakefront deck?
A: Yes, if there is a stair or path we can walk safely. [[Very long carries down to the water may add time to the job]], so tell us about the path when you book.

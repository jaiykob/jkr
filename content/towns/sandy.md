## Local
Sandy is the gateway to Mount Hood and the last town of any size on Highway 26 before the mountain. At about fourteen road miles from our base, it falls in Zone 2. We haul from the newer subdivisions off Bornstedt and Ten Eyck, the homes near downtown, and the rural lots outside town.

Sandy homes take a beating from winter weather, so storm damage cleanups come up every year: fallen branches, broken fencing and soaked carpet from a leak. Summer brings garage cleanouts and the odds and ends from cabins being emptied on the way down the mountain.

## FAQ

Q: Do you take fallen fencing after a storm?

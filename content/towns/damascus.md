## Local
Damascus still has the feel of farm country, with acreage, long gravel drives and outbuildings that have stored things for decades. Most of it sits about seven road miles from our base, inside the home zone, which keeps big rural cleanouts affordable.

The jobs here run bigger than in town: barns full of old feed bins and fencing, shops with broken tools and tires, and sheds that have not been opened since the last owner. We bring the truck down the drive and load from wherever the pile is. [[Tires are an added charge per tire, because the recycler charges us.]]

## FAQ
Q: Can your truck drive down a gravel road to the barn?
A: Usually, yes. If the drive is soft, steep or has a low gate, tell us when you book and send a photo so we know where to park.

Q: Do you take old fencing and T posts?
A: Yes. Roll the wire and stack the posts if you can. [[Rolls of barbed wire must be tied off]] so we can carry them without getting cut.

## Local
Aloha is the big unincorporated stretch between Beaverton and Hillsboro, twenty eight to thirty road miles from our base and in Zone 3. Most homes are single family houses on quiet streets off Farmington Road and Tualatin Valley Highway, many with a driveway full of projects.

Garage and side yard cleanouts top the list in Aloha: broken lawn mowers, old bikes, scrap wood and the furniture that moved out of the house and never moved back in. If it is sitting in the driveway or by the curb, you do not need to be home for us to load it.

## FAQ
Q: Do you take old lawn mowers?
A: Yes. [[Drain the gas and oil first]], or tell us and we will set the mower aside. Push mowers and riding mowers both go.

Q: Can you pick up from the curb in Aloha while I am at work?
A: Yes. Set the items out, add a note about where they are when you book, and we will send photos when they are gone.

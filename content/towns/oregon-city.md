## Local
Oregon City spreads from the historic McLoughlin district by the falls up the bluff to Hilltop and out along Beavercreek Road. All of it is in Zone 2. Homes range from century old houses with tight stairways to big newer lots in the South End and Park Place.

Old houses near downtown mean old attics and cellars, and those are some of our most interesting jobs: trunks, broken furniture, stacks of magazines and the odd cast iron sink. Up the hill, the work looks more like garage cleanouts and remodel leftovers. Either way, we load what you point at and leave the space swept.

## FAQ
Q: Can you clear an attic in an old Oregon City house?
A: Yes. We bring everything down by hand. If the attic is reached by a pull down ladder, tell us so we plan for the extra carrying time.

Q: Do you take a cast iron tub or sink?
A: Yes, if it is already disconnected. [[Cast iron tubs add a heavy item fee]] because they take extra people to lift.

## Local (es)

Subiendo la loma, el trabajo se parece más a vaciar garajes y llevarse lo que sobró de remodelaciones. De cualquier forma, cargamos lo que nos señales y dejamos el lugar barrido.

## Local
Aurora is a small historic town on Highway 99E, thirty six road miles from our base, in Zone 4. Its old colony buildings and antique shops sit alongside farms, nurseries and homes near the airport.

Old homes and antique trade mean we sometimes haul furniture that is simply past repair, along with attic and barn contents from long held properties. Farm loads include old fencing, irrigation pipe and pallets. Anything that still has life in it goes to donation instead of the landfill.

## FAQ
Q: Will you donate old furniture that is still usable?
A: Yes. Usable furniture goes to donation when a center will take it. [[We drop donations at a charity in the metro on our way back]].

Q: Can you haul from a farm near the Aurora airport?
A: Yes. Point us to the piles and tell us where the truck can park. Photos help us bring the right load size.

## Local (es)
Aurora es un pueblo pequeño e histórico sobre la Highway 99E, a treinta y seis millas por carretera de nuestra base, en la Zona 4. Sus viejos edificios de la colonia y sus tiendas de antigüedades conviven con granjas, viveros y casas cerca del aeropuerto.

Las casas antiguas y el comercio de antigüedades hacen que a veces llevemos muebles que ya no tienen arreglo, junto con lo que guardan los áticos y graneros de propiedades de muchos años. Las cargas de granja traen cercas viejas, tubos de riego y tarimas. Lo que todavía sirve va a donación en lugar del relleno sanitario.

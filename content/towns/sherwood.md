## Local
Sherwood is a growing town at the southwest edge of the metro, thirty four road miles from our base, in Zone 4. Most homes are newer subdivisions around Woodhaven and Cedar Creek, with a few older streets near Old Town and farmland just outside.

Newer homes mean fewer basements but full garages. We clear out moving boxes, furniture that did not fit the new floor plan, and the toys and bikes that families outgrow fast. Old Town brings the occasional attic of older finds.

## FAQ

Q: Can you haul bikes and outdoor toys from a Sherwood garage?

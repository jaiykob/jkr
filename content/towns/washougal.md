## Local
Washougal is the last town east on our Washington side before the Gorge, twenty seven road miles from our base, in Zone 3. Homes range from older houses near the downtown and the waterfront to rural lots up Washougal River Road.

Gorge wind and river weather mean storm debris and worn outdoor furniture come up often, along with the usual furniture and appliance pickups. Rural lots up the river road bring shop cleanouts and old fencing. Tell us if your drive is gravel or steep when you book.

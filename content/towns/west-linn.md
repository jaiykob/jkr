## Local
West Linn sits just under twenty road miles from our base, at the far end of Zone 2. Its neighborhoods climb from the old Willamette main street up into the hills, where many homes have long driveways, big decks and room for a boat or two.

Remodels drive much of our West Linn work: old cabinets, carpet pulled up in rolls, fixtures and the packaging from everything new. We also clear out garages ahead of a move. On the hills, we park where the street allows and carry down or up the drive.

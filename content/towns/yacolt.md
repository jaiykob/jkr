## Local
Yacolt is a tiny town in the hills of north Clark County, thirty nine road miles from our base, at the far edge of Zone 4. Most homes are on wooded lots, many off gravel roads, with shops, woodsheds and outbuildings.

Forest living fills outbuildings with old firewood racks, broken tools, scrap metal and furniture moved out of the house years ago. We load from the yard or the shed, and we plan Yacolt trips on the same days as our other north county stops.

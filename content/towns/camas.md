## Local
Camas is twenty three road miles from our base, over the I-205 bridge and east along Highway 14, which puts it in Zone 3. The old mill town downtown is surrounded by newer neighborhoods on Prune Hill and around Lacamas Lake, many with large homes and long driveways.

Families on Prune Hill and in Grass Valley call us for garage cleanouts, remodel leftovers and furniture swaps when a room gets redone. Lake homes add outdoor gear and old dock pieces. Because many Camas streets are hilly, we look at your photos to plan where the truck parks.

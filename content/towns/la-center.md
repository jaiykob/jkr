## Local
La Center is a small town on the East Fork of the Lewis River, thirty eight road miles from our base, in Zone 4. It has a compact downtown, new neighborhoods on the hill, and rural lots along the river.

River properties collect old canoes, outdoor furniture and the remains of past flood seasons, while the new homes on the hill bring garage cleanouts and moving leftovers. Because La Center is one of our longest drives, we group jobs up there on set days.

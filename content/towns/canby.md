## Local
Canby is a farm town on the Willamette, twenty seven road miles from our base down Highway 99E, which places it in Zone 3. Homes range from older houses near downtown to newer subdivisions on the edges and farms with shops and barns.

Farm and nursery country means outbuilding cleanouts: old pallets, worn tarps, broken greenhouse frames and equipment that stopped running years ago. In town, we take the usual furniture, appliances and garage leftovers. Big rural loads often fill a whole truck, so photos of each pile help us bring the right size.

Q: Can you load from a field or a barn in Canby?

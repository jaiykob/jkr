## Local
Vancouver is the biggest city on our Washington side, and the I-205 bridge puts its east side closer to Gresham than most people expect. Fisher's Landing, Cascade Park and the Mill Plain corridor fall in Zone 2, while downtown, Hazel Dell, Felida and Salmon Creek are in Zone 3.

Clark County homes tend to have roomy garages and side yards, so full garage cleanouts are common here, along with old hot tubs, swing sets and sheds that have seen better days. [[We follow Washington rules for where hauled items go, which differ in places from Oregon's.]]

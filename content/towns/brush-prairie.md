## Local
Brush Prairie and nearby Hockinson are rural Clark County, about twenty five road miles from our base, in Zone 3. Homes sit on bigger lots with pastures, barns and shops, and plenty of families keep horses or a few animals.

Most of our Brush Prairie work is outdoor and outbuilding cleanouts: old hay, broken feeders, rusted gates, scrap lumber and equipment that has quit. We load from wherever the pile is, as long as the truck can reach it on solid ground.

## Local
Corbett is fourteen road miles from our base, up the hill from Troutdale on the old Columbia River Highway, and it is in Zone 2. It is a rural place with farms, big wooded lots and homes with sweeping views of the Gorge.

Wind is a fact of life up here, and storm cleanups are a regular job for us in Corbett: downed limbs, broken sheds and outdoor furniture that took flight. Farms bring their own loads, from old equipment parts to fencing and lumber. We bring the truck as close to the pile as the property allows.

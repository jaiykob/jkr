## Local
Battle Ground is about twenty eight road miles from our base, up Highway 503 north of Vancouver, in Zone 3. It has a small downtown, newer subdivisions on every side, and acreage out toward Cherry Grove and Meadow Glade.

New subdivisions here mean many families with full garages and leftover moving boxes, while the acreage brings barn and shop cleanouts, old hay feeders and scrap metal. We fit Battle Ground jobs in with our other Clark County stops, so booking a few days out helps.

# Facebook group posts

Posts for town and neighborhood groups (the list is `../facebook-groups.md`).
Read each group's rules first: many allow business posts only on one day a
week. A person posts; the helpers only draft (`agent/skills/`). Facts are in
`README.md`.

## Notes on what works here

- Answer every comment the same day, by name.
- A before and after photo of a real job (with permission) beats any wording.
- Do not repost the same text in many groups the same day; groups flag it.
- Never post a price in the post; prices change and the site is always right.

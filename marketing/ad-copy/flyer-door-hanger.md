## Distribution notes

- GUESS* 25 to 40 door hangers per job, on the job's street and the next one over.
- Never in mailboxes (federal law); hang on the door handle.
- Note the street and date in the post log in `../facebook-groups.md`, so you
  can tell which streets call back.
- Print the QR code to the quote page itself, not the homepage: one fewer tap.

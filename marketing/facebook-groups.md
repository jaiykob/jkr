# Facebook groups: where to post

Town and neighborhood groups across our travel zones, worked from the
closest zone outward (closest jobs pay best). The posts themselves are in
`ad-copy/facebook-group-posts.md`. Call or text line for every post:
(925) 408-6053.

GUESS* Group names change and new ones appear; search for each kind below and
write the real names into the post log as you join them.

## Rules of engagement (read once, then never break them)

1. Read the group rules before the first post. Many allow business posts only
   on one weekday, or not at all; respect it.
2. Introduce yourself once per group (post 1). After that, only reply when
   someone asks for a hauler, or post on the allowed day.
3. A person posts. The helpers draft; they never post or message on their own
   (a ban costs the whole group).
4. No prices in posts. The site is always right; a post goes stale.
5. Answer every comment the same day.

## Tier 1: home zone towns (no travel charge, best margin)

Search for: "Gresham community", "Troutdale neighbors", "Fairview Oregon",
"Happy Valley Oregon", "Boring Damascus", "Clackamas community",
"East Portland neighbors". Post 1, then post 4 whenever someone asks.

## Tier 2: Zone 2 towns

Search for: "Milwaukie neighbors", "Oregon City community", "West Linn",
"Sandy Oregon", "Gladstone", "Estacada", "Vancouver WA east side". The
travel fee here is small; lead with the photo quote.

## Tier 3: moving, Buy Nothing and garage sale groups

Search for: "Buy Nothing Gresham" (and each town), "Portland moving sale",
"Vancouver garage sales". Here the angle is what did not sell or did not fit
the moving truck: post 2.

## Tier 4: landlords and property managers

Search for: "Portland landlords", "Oregon landlord network",
"Clark County landlords". Post 5. One monthly pickup account is worth many
single hauls.

## Adjacent channels (not Facebook, same job)

- Nextdoor (`ad-copy/short-form.md`): one business page post a week.
- Google Business Profile posts (`gbp-optimization.md`): one a week.
- Realtors and estate sale companies: a short intro email offering the day
  after a sale or before listing photos.
